## LinkedIn draft (company page, for a future Friday synthesis)

330 days.

That is the median age of the 6,036 Nashville building permit applications still unissued on November 3, 2026 - the pending side of the queue we measured from the other direction earlier this week, where the median completed wait among issued permits was 29 days. Both figures are from Metro's own layers, pulled the same day. The stock is not the flow: applications that resolve leave the queue, so what accumulates is the residue. 47.5% of the pending stock is over a year old.

The cohort view is the sharper finding. Of applications entered in 2024 and 2025 and observable in either layer, 12.9% are still unissued - about one in eight. For commercial new construction the residue is 37.1%. The applications layer records no status, so the published queue cannot separate live projects from dead filings; the brief's ask is that it start doing so.
